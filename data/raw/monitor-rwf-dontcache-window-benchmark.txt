Definitions:

- classify: first preadv2 call, ending with EAGAIN.
- cleanup: from EAGAIN return until the page is absent again.
- total/window: from the start of Monitor until absence is restored; this is
  an upper bound on the contamination interval.
- full sequence: includes the leading Flush as well.
- NOWAIT then Flush waits until the asynchronous read is complete before the
  final Flush. An immediate Flush is not a stable cleanup, as the race test
  demonstrates.

The backing block becomes warm during repetition, so these figures measure the
best-case kernel/mechanism cost. Cold storage, contention and scheduling can
make the tail substantially longer.
