Interpretation:

- The first return still classifies the initial state: EAGAIN for absent and
  one byte for present.
- On an absent page, the first call starts I/O and marks the new folio for
  drop-behind. The page is transiently cached.
- Repeating the call after I/O completion reads the marked folio and removes
  it, restoring the initial absent state.
- A page that was already cached is not newly marked drop-behind and remains
  cached after the probe.
